# Holmgard and beyond — Game Project (second project, after CM)

Viking-era merchant-crew survival journey. Original pitch (Nov 2026 weighing): Viking merchant/mercenary journey, Birka → Volga route → Baghdad, light resource management, Journey-to-the-West structure, strongly stylized flat art. **REFRAMED Nov 19, 2026: Oregon Trail structure** — survival is the core ("survival is more important", his decision), trading reduced to a survival means, not a profit engine. Named party members (sicken/defect/die), random events per leg, permadeath run-based replayability. Episodic-release candidate. Parked while CM ships.
