# Leg 3 — The Road to Miklagard · design brief v1

Status: DRAFT awaiting Andrzej's sign-off (round 36 draft). Everything below follows the locked canon: Aifur-class hull, ~26 km/day pacing knob, spring/one-season clock (no winters), finale at Constantinople.

## Premise

Leg 3 starts the moment the v0.2 Holmgard settlement lands (day counter continues from the crossing, ~day 44+). The venture: cash out and re-fit at Holmgard, run the water-road south in the wake of the convoys, and bring a cargo through to the imperial market at Miklagard. Finale: late summer, ~day 110–125.

## Skeleton (his call, round 35: five hubs, four legs)

| # | Hub | Role | Design-tuned travel days |
|---|-----|------|------|
| 1 | Holmgard | start hub: sell crossing cargo, load southbound venture, recruit | — |
| 2 | Gnezdovo | waystation hub: re-provision, tar and caulk, road news | ~24 (10 up the Lovat + 14 incl. the watershed portage) |
| 3 | Kiev | the big hub: June gathering absorbs the Vitichev muster (2–3 day wait), hire, feast, commit to the rapids | ~18 downstream |
| 4 | Cherson | imperial gate: St Mamas white bluffs, the summons wait (2–5 days), escort | ~26 (rapid runs + Pecheneg shore + delta) |
| 5 | Constantinople | finale: imperial market, silk for silver, payoff + end screen | ~8–9 sea (incl. Mesembria landfall, St Aitherios re-rig as arrival flavor) |

Total ~80–95 travel days plus waits, consistent with the Aifur evidence. Day counts are design-tuned on the 26 km/day anchor and labeled as such in-game (per the standing dishonsty... honesty rule).

## Clock and gates (the replacement for the convoy clock)

- Gate A (soft): Lovat low water, near Holmgard — late start already costs hull/portage time.
- Gate B (hard): the June gathering leaves Kiev — miss it and you descend the rapids alone (rapids hazards sharply harder, no convoy mutual aid).
- Gate C (hard): Pecheneg peace — the state variable of the leg. Peace = the DAI sequence plays as written. War = ambush events at the portages, the 972-blocked-return template as fail state.
- Gate D (hard): the Black Sea season — arrive at Cherson too late (day ~120+) and the summons never comes; fail text "the sea closes."

## Event rosters per leg (draft lists)

Holmgard → Gnezdovo: Lovat shallows (low-water event, reuse thin-flood mechanics); the watershed portage as a choice-cluster (wheels built on site / pay Slavic teamsters with wheels); first silver-hoard rumors (flavor); storm on the Ilmen.

Gnezdovo → Kiev: downriver days (fast when winds favor); tribute-town fish markets; Krivich ferrymen; a Chud kinsman from the crossing reappearing (crew texture); sick-day and seam events reskinned.

Kiev → Cherson: the seven rapids as set pieces — three passage modes each per the DAI (warp through / run under oar / portage round), Nenasytets as the big one (cargo walked ~6 miles, boats dragged, chained captives risk); Pecheneg parley (buy horned cattle, sell iron, or give offense); the cock sacrifice at Khortytsia (omenevent, morale swing); delta reeds and channels; first sight of Greek brick on the Cherson bluffs.

Cherson → Miklagard: the summons wait event; imperial escort join; coastal storm; St Aitherios re-rig; the embrace of the Golden Horn.

## Hubs, briefly

- Gnezdovo: all-timber art rule (no stone — Run 6 finding). Market: smoked fish, honey/wax, tar (a caulk-and-seal hull heal), freight contracts ON (2 dirhams per unit paid at Kiev). Road news: Pecheneg peace state revealed here for planning.
- Kiev: the June gathering gates departure; hire (best pool of the leg); feast; OPTION: hull-switch mechanic (experimental verdict: Scandinavians did not take their own hulls past the rivers).
- Cherson: sell nothing — the treaty market is at the capital; pay the escort fee, buy provisions, hear the summons.

## Economy & scoring

- Silver thins across leg 3 (tolls, provisions, hires); destination pay is in miliarhesia/silk handled at the finale.
- Freight contracts repeat at Holmgard and Gnezdovo (same 2/unit terms).
- Finale settlement at Miklagard: marks = venture dirhams + silk bought (silk as score artifact) + crew intact + days ahead of day ~120.
- New skippers'-board destination tag: Miklagard.

## Fail states

Rapids hull loss (at zero, with cargo); Pecheneg massacre at a portage (blocked-return template, war state only); the sea closes at Cherson; starvation; sickness unchecked. All one-season, no winters anywhere.

## Open calls for sign-off

- D1 pacing: compressed 26 km/day real-anchored days (~90) vs game-tuned shorter (~70)? My rec: real-anchored, mirrors v0.2 leg length feel.
- D2 Pecheneg peace: peace state as fixed start (911/912, attested low contact) with randomization only in ambush pressure, or player-causable war via bad parleys? My rec: the latter — parleys matter.
- D3 Kiev hull switch: in v0.3 or park? My rec: park; the Aifur canon lets you keep her.
- D4 captives: retired at the Holmgard settlement (leg 3 cargo = furs, wax, honey, amber), or continue south (DAI chains are attested)? My rec: continue — the Nenasytets walk with chained captives is the most documented image of the whole road.
- D5 finale format: single settlement screen (like v0.2) plus a silk-buying beat before it? My rec: yes, the silk beat is the payoff moment.
- D6 day-numbering: continuous from Birka (finale ~day 115 = mid-Aug) or restart the count per leg? My rec: continuous — one season, one ledger.
