# Holmgard and beyond

A Viking merchant-crew route-survival game in the spirit of Oregon Trail. Sail and row from Birka to Miklagard (Constantinople) c. 911 AD: manage rations, silver, hull and crew spirits across a 90-day trading season, four legs, three water-roads and one Greek sea crossing.

**Play:** https://ap-vibe.github.io/holmgard/

- Single-file HTML game, works offline after first load (installable on phones)
- The skippers’ board records every finished run on your device
- Art: HD-2D "fake pixel" dioramas, sprites and scenes
- Design and code: AP-Vibe (Vibe / Mistral AI) for Andrzej Pietras

Every number, crew name and price is design-tuned from research runs on the Rus trade route; the game is fiction, not a history lesson.
