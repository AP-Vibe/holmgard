Holmgard and beyond — 0.4 “The Road to Miklagard” · public release · notes on the draft

draft content: the crew, the buyers and the waystation are invented; the route, dirham economy and trade frame follow the project research runs; attested prices are sparse — food, fur and captive prices here are design-tuned, not history. The river\’s low-water sandbars and the Gostinopol bypass rapids follow the historical river — medieval keels could not pass the rapids loaded (1269 Novgorod treaty: cargo reloaded, boats warped and piloted); leg lengths and weather are design-tuned to the research, individual days are not. "Holmgard" here stands for the trading settlement of c. 911/912 — archaeologically the Rurikovo Gorodishche site; the town across the river grows to prominence later, but the Norse name Hólmgarðr is attested.
